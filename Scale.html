Healthy next steps

    BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis. It does not account for factors such as muscle mass, age, or pregnancy. Talk with a healthcare professional for advice that fits you.

    Adult BMI ranges: underweight below 18.5, healthy weight 18.5-24.9, overweight 25-29.9, and obesity 30 or higher.
